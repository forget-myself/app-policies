English

This Privacy Policy describes how the independent developer operating under the name Kossnei Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Lucky Mahjong: Tile Match (the "App"). For privacy questions, contact kossnei88@gmail.com.

The current release is a locally played tile-matching game. We do not operate an in-app account system or a server that receives your gameplay records. The current release does not enable advertising SDKs, third-party usage analytics, or automatic cloud crash reporting.

1. Information processed on your device

The App stores game progress, unlocked levels, scores, item balances, activity and reward records, and sound or gameplay preferences in the App's local storage. It also stores packaged game-content files in a local cache. These records support game operation and continuity; the App does not send them to us.

The App uses device capabilities, such as screen size, touch input and audio settings, to display and run the game. We do not receive a device profile or link these capabilities to an identity. The current release does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS, or call history, and does not collect advertising identifiers for advertising or tracking.

2. Messages you choose to send

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide. We use them to respond to support or privacy requests. Please send only information needed for your request. Do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information, share gameplay records with advertisers, or use personal information for targeted advertising in the current release. Email providers process support messages to deliver and store email. We may disclose information supplied in a support request when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Google Play, Android, and your device manufacturer may independently process information to distribute the App, provide platform security or app protection, and perform backup or other platform functions. Those providers' processing is governed by their own terms, settings and policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

4. This policy website and external links

This policy is hosted using GitHub Pages. Loading this website sends standard web-request information, such as your IP address, to the hosting provider. GitHub describes its processing in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or cookies to this policy page. Opening an external link or sending email also involves the browser, email service or destination provider.

5. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game records remain on your device until you clear the App's storage or remove the App, subject to Android's storage and backup behavior. To reset local data, use Android Settings → Apps → Lucky Mahjong: Tile Match → Storage → Clear storage/data. This permanently resets local progress and settings. No in-app account needs to be deleted.

Depending on your Android version and settings, the operating system may back up and later restore app data. Manage or delete any platform backup through your device or Google account settings; clearing local App data does not necessarily delete a platform backup.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, handle follow-up questions, and meet applicable legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us, email kossnei88@gmail.com. We will handle your request as required by applicable law. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete gameplay data that exists only on your device.

6. Security

Local data is stored within Android's app storage protections. We use reasonable measures to protect support correspondence and limit its use to the purposes described here. No device, storage system or method of transmission is completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a support message, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

8. International processing and policy updates

Email, website-hosting and platform providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence under their own policies. We may update this policy when the App or our data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version. If advertising, analytics, accounts or other data-collecting features are added, we will update the policy and provide any notices or consent required before that processing begins.
